# Stage 31 — role transfer and authored ambiguity

Stage 30's late English score measures whether the response expresses a
recoverable, correct owner–keeper pair. Omission or poetic indeterminacy must
be recorded separately from an explicit reversal of a user-stated role.
Literary continuity is reviewed independently: a staircase acquiring a
reputation or a river interviewing a basement belongs to Dubrovsky's voice.

## Frozen protocol

Zero optimizer steps. Keep stage 7 selected. Freeze all body and expert tensors,
verify their logical byte hashes, and retain native BF16 production sampling.
Compare stage 7 (expert correction disabled), stage-28 step 24, and both
stage-30 step-24 endpoints (contrast weights 1 and 12).

Select two existing English training pairs: one ownership gift/loan pair and
one custody return pair, each with two source histories. Cross three factors:

- Original versus new names, replaced simultaneously in user facts and labels.
- Original versus new authored assistant history, with user messages unchanged.
- Generic object reference versus its exact name, with the rest of the question unchanged.

This produces 32 positions per arm, 128 complete answers. Both members and all
eight variants of a family use the same generation seed. The two families use
different seeds. These are controlled transfers from training pairs; they are
not a newly held-out acceptance set. Names change tokenization as well as
familiarity. The two scene versions differ in imagery and length.

At each position, score the actual changing role value against its paired
alternative in native BF16, FP32 head projection, and FP32 correction addition.
Swap only the bank after the same full prefix, preserving the working state.
Check that assistant-history substitutions preserve the isolated user bank
bit for bit. Record values with their token counts and full prompt hashes.

Revisit all eight late English recalls from the stage-30 weight-12 candidate:
exact replay, explicit object reference at the same prefix, and a natural
clarification appended after the saved actual answer. The clarification names
the object and asks whose it is and who keeps it; it supplies no person's name
or corrective fact. Keep the original sampling seed across these three
conditions. This adds 24 answers, for 152 in total. Verify exact replays by
token IDs. An appended clarification has a longer history and a new question;
report its result as conversational recovery, separately from matched-prefix
query substitutions.

## Contextual reading

Read every answer with its actual preceding history. Record recoverable roles,
explicit contrary assignments, omitted roles, and ambiguous literary assertions.
Credit correct roles embedded in absurdism. Do not infer forgotten memory from
an unexpressed role or infer a literal transfer from a metaphor alone. A direct
lender-to-borrower reversal is a concrete conflict with the given transaction.
Record scene continuation and language quality separately from role accuracy.

Original stage-30 transcripts remain immutable. A changed interpretation is
documented with the original text and its surrounding turns. No automatic
keyword score chooses a checkpoint. English is the focus of this diagnostic;
Russian behavior is not measured anew here.

## Execution

Run `probe_role_transfer.py` with the pinned base, private sealed cases and
checkpoint manifest. Private source text, responses and expert deltas remain
outside git. Unit checks cover independent factor changes, bounded simultaneous
renaming, source immutability, and restoration of the base ablation after an error.
Collect and hash-check raw results before releasing the owned Secure Cloud
GPU; archive the full contextual review after reading.

## Preparation correction before generation

The first launch stopped at the strict shared-prefix check before producing
answers. Alternative teacher answers of different token lengths were previously
evaluated as separate complete sequences. Stage 31 now computes their common
Qwen prefix once, clones its cache, and advances each answer suffix token by
token. The exact prefix assertion remains in place. The audit records the
legacy full-sequence difference alongside the shared-cache check for every row.
A tiny Qwen test verifies both cache forks against independent cached rollouts.
The failed launch, its inputs, and its logs are retained. Cases, seeds, frozen
checkpoints, and generation settings are unchanged.
